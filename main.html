Goa


Goa is one of the most popular holiday destinations in India, known for its beautiful beaches, nightlife, Portuguese heritage, and delicious food.

Top 5 Attractions

  1. Baga Beach - Famous for water sports and nightlife.
  2. Calangute Beach - One of the most popular beaches in Goa.
  3. Basilica of Bom Jesus - A famous historical church.
  4. Dudhsagar Falls - A spectacular waterfall surrounded by nature.
  5. Fort Aguada - A historic Portuguese fort with beautiful views.

Must-Try Foods

  • Goan Fish Curry
  • Prawn Balchao
  • Chicken Xacuti
  • Bebinca
  • Goan Sausage
